Examples

Example data and settings

Five simulated datasets, seven worked studies and the outputs they produce. The datasets and settings files are also inside the app download, in the Example Data folder, so you can reproduce each output on your own computer.

The data

Five simulated datasets

No real patient appears in any file: every value was generated by a computer model with a fixed seed, and patients and eyes carry codes only. Each dataset shows one part of the app. Every file has a README sheet with the generating model, every true parameter, the seed and a column dictionary with units and notation, and the generators ship in the validation folder of the app, so the files can be written again and checked.

Dataset_A_two_techniques.xlsx

A · Two techniques

404 eyes of 215 patients, PRK versus LASIK. Decimal acuity, minus-cylinder refraction, follow-up visits at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months with drop-out, and the 6-month visit as the endpoint.

LASIK: 194 eyes of 105 patients; PRK: 210 eyes of 110 patients.

Shows the basic workflow: two groups, the standard graphs, the Publication Table, the tabs for mEYEstro and AstigMATIC, the stability curve and the Preview only run.

Settings: Settings_A_two_techniques.json (preset Example - A Two techniques)

Dataset_B_notation.xlsx

B · Notation harmonization

339 eyes of 190 patients at two centers. Cylinders typed in plus and minus notation in the same columns (about 40% plus, targets too), and each acuity recorded twice: as a logMAR line score and as a Snellen fraction with letter suffixes (“20/25-2”).

Center 1: 170 eyes of 96 patients; Center 2: 169 eyes of 94 patients.

Shows the built-in conversion: every plus cylinder is transposed to minus cylinder, and the run log counts the cylinders transposed in each refraction; the README sheet says which rows were plus. Acuity is analyzed in logMAR and shown as the Snellen geometric mean.

Settings: Settings_B_notation_logMAR.json and Settings_B_notation_Snellen.json (presets Example - B Notation (logMAR) and (Snellen)). The app reads one acuity notation per settings file, so the same eyes come with two.

Dataset_C_four_platforms.xlsx

C · Four platforms

457 eyes of 250 patients treated with four fictitious excimer platforms, each with a documented true profile of accuracy, predictability, astigmatic correction and safety. Acuity as ETDRS letter scores.

Platform 1: 134 eyes of 74 patients; Platform 2: 95 eyes of 54 patients; Platform 3: 116 eyes of 61 patients; Platform 4: 112 eyes of 61 patients.

Shows the comparison of several groups on one chart, each in its own color: an overall test on every panel and each platform against Platform 1 with Holm-adjusted P values.

Settings: Settings_C_four_platforms.json (preset Example - C Four platforms)

Dataset_D_astigmatism.xlsx

D · Astigmatism

392 eyes of 210 patients, KLEx versus LASIK, with 0.75 to 4.00 D of preoperative cylinder, mostly with the rule. KLEx undercorrects and rotates the treatment by design, and eyes with high cylinder have a planned residual cylinder (a target that is not plano).

KLEx: 228 eyes of 123 patients; LASIK: 164 eyes of 87 patients.

Shows the JCRS 2025 astigmatism analysis: plus-cylinder double-angle plots of the preoperative astigmatism, SIA, prediction error and postoperative astigmatism with centroids, total SD and 95% regions; the centroid tests; the Alpins graphs; the AstigMATIC sheet and the input files for the WHWK functions.

Settings: Settings_D_astigmatism.json (preset Example - D Astigmatism)

Download dataset D (53 KB) Settings of dataset D
Dataset_E_confounded_cohorts.xlsx

E · Confounded cohorts

518 eyes of 275 patients, a cohort with a simulated systemic condition versus controls, both eyes of most patients. Age, preoperative spherical equivalent and pachymetry drive both cohort membership and the outcomes. True effects: postoperative spherical equivalent −0.20 D, odds ratio 2.0 for dry-eye symptoms, no effect on postoperative cylinder.

Condition: 194 eyes of 106 patients; Control: 324 eyes of 169 patients.

Shows patient-clustered tests, covariate-adjusted GEE and regression, propensity-score matching of eyes and of patients (both eyes kept together), the love plot and the matched tables, and the crude, adjusted and matched estimates beside the truth. The README sheet also gives the app's matched results, including the covariate the 1:1 match leaves slightly unbalanced.

Settings: Settings_E_confounded_eye_matching.json and Settings_E_confounded_patient_matching.json (presets Example - E Confounded cohorts (eye matching) and (patient matching))

Start here

New to the app? Load Example Data\Dataset_A_two_techniques.xlsx, click the preset Example - A Two techniques, tick Preview only and press Run. The tutorials walk through the same steps.

The validation folder of the app also holds the astigmatism validation workbook, built to test the double-angle plots and the astigmatism statistics.

How the datasets are laid out

ColumnsWhat they hold
PatientID, EyeID, EyeCodes only, no names or dates. Two eyes of one person share a PatientID; one row per eye.
Procedure, Center, Platform or CohortThe column the groups are defined from.
Age_Years, SexPatient-level baseline variables.
Preop_Sphere_D, Preop_Cylinder_D, Preop_Axis_Deg, Preop_SEQ_DPreoperative refraction in 0.25 D steps (a blank axis when the cylinder is 0) and the spherical equivalent, SEQ = sphere + cylinder/2 exactly. Dataset B mixes plus and minus cylinder; the others use minus cylinder.
Preop_UDVA, Preop_CDVA (and _logMAR, _Snellen, _Letters versions)Visual acuity on chart lines: decimal (A, D, E), logMAR and Snellen (B) or ETDRS letters (C). An uncorrected acuity poorer than the chart's last line is recorded as CF (counting fingers), which the app sets to missing and counts in the run log.
Target_…The intended refraction: plano, a myopic monovision target in one eye of some older patients, or a planned residual cylinder (B, D).
FollowUp_Days, Postop_…Days to the 6-month visit (blank when it was missed), and refraction, acuity and the other outcomes at that visit.
Visit1_… to Visit4_… (A)Days after surgery and refraction at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, for the stability curve.

Your own file does not need these names or all of these columns. See how to lay out your data.

The settings

Seven worked studies

A preset is one small file that holds every setting of a study. Each example is in the app as an Example - … preset, and in the Example Data folder as a settings file (the same preset as a .json file). Each runs on its own dataset with the 1.2.0 defaults.

Settings_A_two_techniques.json

Dataset A - two techniques (PRK vs LASIK)

Two groups (PRK, LASIK) at the 6-month endpoint: the summary tables with patient-clustered tests, the standard graphs, the Publication Table, the mEYEstro and AstigMATIC tabs and the stability curve.

The basic workflow on a two-group dataset: decimal acuity analyzed in logMAR, minus-cylinder refraction, the 6-month window as a filter, the stability over 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, and the export tabs for mEYEstro and AstigMATIC. Run it with 'Preview only' to see the results before saving them.

Two groups: PRK, LASIK; data: Dataset_A_two_techniques.xlsx

Settings_B_notation_Snellen.json

Dataset B - notation harmonization (Snellen columns)

Two centers; refraction typed in mixed plus and minus cylinder notation (targets too); acuity read from the Snellen columns (VA notation: Snellen).

The built-in harmonization: every plus cylinder (preoperative, target, postoperative) is transposed to minus cylinder before the analysis, the astigmatism vectors are built in plus-cylinder format whatever the typed form, and acuity recorded as Snellen fractions with letter suffixes is converted to logMAR (counting fingers set to missing); tables and figures show acuity as the Snellen geometric mean with the logMAR mean and SD.

Settings_E_confounded_eye_matching.json

Dataset E - confounded cohorts, eye-level matching

Condition versus Control with measured confounding (age, preop SEQ, pachymetry): crude tables, covariate-adjusted GEE and regression, and 1:1 propensity-score matching of eyes.

Patient-clustered tests, the adjusted GEE, propensity-score matching (nearest neighbor 1:1 without replacement, caliper 0.2 SD of the logit of the score) with the love plot and the matched tables, and crude, adjusted and matched estimates side by side against the documented true effects (SEQ -0.20 D, dry-eye OR 2.0, no cylinder effect). On this file, 1:1 matching of eyes with a caliper of 0.2 SD of the logit leaves age at a standardized difference of 0.11 (eyes; beyond the love plot's 0.1 line, and the PSM sheet marks it 'no'); the covariate-adjusted matched GEE (-0.21 D for postop SEQ, true -0.20 D) removes that residual imbalance. The caliper is the default and was not tuned to balance this sample.

Two groups: Control, Condition; data: Dataset_E_confounded_cohorts.xlsx

Use one in the app

Follow the first-run steps: load the dataset, then click the Example - … preset you want. To open a downloaded settings file instead, click Load config JSON in the sidebar.

Or run the analysis without the interface

From the app folder, in a command prompt:

"C:\Program Files\R\R-4.5.2\bin\Rscript.exe" --vanilla engine\run_pipeline.R "Example Data\Settings_A_two_techniques.json"

The settings use paths relative to the app folder. Results appear in Example Output. Keep the app folder near the drive root, such as C:\StandardEyes: Windows cannot write a file whose full path passes 260 characters.
